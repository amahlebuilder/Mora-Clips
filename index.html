01 / WHY SHORT-FORM

Your best moments shouldn't stop at one upload.

A single podcast, stream or video can hold a lot of moments worth sharing. We help turn those moments into focused edits that can meet people where they already spend their time.

01 — DISCOVERY

Reach beyond your regulars

Give new people an easy entry point into your work with concise moments that make them curious about the full story.

02 — CONSISTENCY

Keep showing up

Build a useful bank of content from recordings you have already made, without having to create from zero for every post.

03 — STORYTELLING

Make the point land

Strong hooks, considered pacing, readable captions and clean framing help each clip communicate one clear idea.

We focus on quality and consistency. Views, reach and virality depend on many factors and are never guaranteed.

02 / THE MORA MISSION

More life from the work you've already made.

We believe good ideas deserve more than one moment to find their people.

Mora Clips exists to make short-form editing more accessible to creators and businesses. We take the time to find the human moments — the insight, the laugh, the reaction, the story — then shape them into clear, platform-ready videos. Not clips for the sake of clips. Content with a reason to exist.

03 / THE PROCESS

From long video to ready-to-post.

A simple, collaborative workflow. You stay focused on creating; we take care of the agreed editing work.

STEP 01

Send the source

Share your recording or source link, plus any direction, references or moments you already have in mind.

STEP 02

We find the cuts

We review the material and identify strong stories, useful insights and moments worth turning into clips.

STEP 03

We shape the edit

We edit for vertical viewing with considered pacing, captions and framing based on your brief.

STEP 04

Ready for your channels

Your finished files are delivered for publishing across the platforms included in your plan.

06 / GOOD TO KNOW

Before we press record.

What kind of content can you work with?

Podcasts, interviews, talking-head videos, streams, gaming content, creator videos and other long-form recordings. We confirm suitability and scope before starting.

Do I need to own the footage?

You must own or have permission to use the source footage, audio, music, guest appearances and any third-party material. We may decline material where rights are unclear.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery timing depends on package size, source length and the editing brief. We confirm a realistic schedule before accepting payment and beginning production.

Can you guarantee views or viral clips?

No. We can focus on strong editing, clear storytelling and platform-ready formatting, but audience response is never guaranteed.

How do I get started?

Email us with your content type, a link to the source material, the package you're considering and your preferred timeline. We'll reply to discuss the fit and next steps.
